Instructions

Using OMR Checker

Set the paper up once, then every sheet takes a few seconds. No photo, key or mark ever leaves the phone.

Before the class
  1. Add the subject on the Subjects tab — for example “Bangla 1st paper” or “Class 9 Science”.
  2. Add the exam inside that subject and pick the question count that matches the sheet you are about to print.
  3. Mark the answer key on the Key tab. Tap A–D for each question, or paste the whole key as 2,1,3,4… and press Apply.
  4. Set the marks if questions are not all worth one mark — type the mark beside the question before saving.
  5. Save to phone. The key is stored in the phone's database, so it is ready for the next paper too.
Printing the sheet

Two ways to get a sheet. The Print tab builds one from scratch with your school's logo, name and address printed at the top; an exam can also print its own sheet. Both use the production design: 15 questions per row with A–D stacked under every question, and the Student ID, Subject Code and Total Marks boxes down the right side.

15 20 25 30 40 45 50 60
Print at 100% / actual size on A4 with “fit to page” switched off. If the printer shrinks the page, the bubbles move and the sheet reads badly. Only sheets printed from this app are read — every exam uses the same 60-slot sheet, and an exam with fewer questions leaves the lower rows empty.
Scanning a paper
  1. Lay the sheet on a flat, evenly lit surface. Daylight or a ceiling light is enough; avoid a shadow falling across the answer area.
  2. Fill the frame. The pink border of the answer table should sit inside the photo with a little margin, and the page should not be tilted much.
  3. Use a dark pen or pencil and fill the bubble fully. A faint tick reads as “review”.
  4. Check the marked photo before writing the total — every bubble the app read is circled on the picture.
What the colours mean
Correct — matches the key Wrong — a different option is filled Key — the right answer, drawn for comparison Double — two bubbles filled, no mark given Review — the mark is faint or unclear

A blank question scores nothing. Only bubbles darker than the printed ring count as filled, so smudges and printing shadows are ignored.

When something goes wrong
The photo is not read at all

Usually the answer table is outside the photo or the page is too tilted. Take the picture straight above the sheet, with the whole pink border visible and space around it.

Answers come out wrong or “review”

Light pencil marks and half-filled bubbles are the usual cause. Fill each bubble fully with a dark pen. If only a few answers look wrong, check the marked photo to see which bubble the app actually measured.

The live overlay jumps between sheets

Move the phone slowly and let the progress bar reach 100% before lifting it. Show the next sheet only after the total appears.

The total does not match the paper

Check the question count of the exam — a 40-question exam read against a 30-question sheet cannot total correctly. The count must match the sheet you printed.

Where are my subjects and marks kept?

In the phone's own app database. They survive closing the app and do not need a network. Deleting a subject deletes its exams and keys with it.
